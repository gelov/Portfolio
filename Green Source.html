Green Source

A shopping companion that shows the environmental story behind every product, so people can make more sustainable choices at the shelf.

Role
UI/UX Design
Platform
Mobile app
Year
—
Client
—

The brief

Most shoppers want to buy more sustainably, but the impact of a product is hidden behind the label. Green Source needed to turn provenance data, such as carbon emitted, water used, plastic packaging and the distance food travels, into something people could understand in the few seconds they spend choosing at the shelf.

What I designed

I designed the app around a simple loop: scan a product, compare it with similar products, make the better choice, and build better habits over time. Every product shows the same three icons for carbon, plastic and water, so impact can be read at a glance. Deeper screens map the product’s journey from farm to store, and a personal dashboard tracks shopping habits with champion and enemy products.
