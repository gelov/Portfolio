EBI.AI

A website for a UK company that helps businesses launch AI customer assistants. It was designed to turn visitors into sign-ups and sales calls.

Role
Web & UI Design
Platform
Website
Year
2022–2026
Client
EBI.AI

The brief

EBI.AI builds AI assistants that answer customer enquiries around the clock for organisations from Legal & General to Barking and Dagenham Council. The site had to explain a technical product to non-technical buyers and prove it works. It also had to offer two clear paths: launch your own assistant in minutes, or hire the team to do it for you.

What I designed

I designed the website around an underwater illustration world that makes AI feel friendly rather than cold. The page opens with a quick email sign-up, then builds the case step by step: client and partner logos, the 533% ROI result, a four-step pay-as-you-grow explainer, industry examples and client quotes. Sign-up prompts repeat down the page, so visitors can start whenever they’re ready.
