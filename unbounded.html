Unbounded

A meditation app for building a daily practice, with timed sessions, progress tracking and groups that keep each other going.

Role
UI/UX Design
Platform
Mobile app
Year
—
Client
Peak Leadership

The brief

Starting to meditate is easy; keeping it up is the hard part. Unbounded needed to help people build a steady habit, with sessions shaped to their routine, clear signs of progress, and the support of a coach and fellow meditators, while the app itself stayed calm and out of the way.

What I designed

I designed a quiet, dark interface built around a single Start ring. Around it sit tools for custom sessions with bells, timed segments and rest, plus reminders that sync to the calendar. Stats track streaks, total days and hours meditated. Groups, a coach and shared milestones turn the practice into something people do together.
