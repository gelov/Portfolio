AI Studio

EBI.AI’s own platform, where businesses build, launch and manage their AI customer assistants.

Role
UI/UX Design
Platform
Web app
Year
2022–2026
Client
EBI.AI

The brief

AI Studio is where EBI.AI customers build and run their AI assistants, from councils answering residents’ questions to retailers handling orders. Its users are customer service teams, not engineers. The product had to make a complex system of conversation flows, content, live-chat handovers and performance data feel approachable and safe to manage day to day.

What I designed

I designed the web app and carried EBI.AI’s underwater world into the product, so it feels part of the same brand. The dashboard puts what matters first: requests this month, how often the assistant understood and helped, the most asked questions, the busiest times and live-chat staffing, alongside a preview of the assistant itself. Further screens cover editing flows and content, reviewing conversations, styling the assistant, and signing up and logging in.
