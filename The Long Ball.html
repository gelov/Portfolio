The Long Ball

A mobile prediction game billed as the most addictive football prediction game in the world. Fans call every Premier League scoreline and compete with friends.

Role
UI/UX Design
Platform
iOS & Android
Year
2020
Client
The Long Ball

The brief

The Long Ball wanted a game fans would come back to every matchweek. Players predict each scoreline, choose bankers, make season-long picks and rank against friends, their continent and the world. The rules run deep, so the challenge was making all of it feel quick and fun on a phone.

What I designed

I designed the app’s interface for iOS and Android, from a brand-led login to the core loop. That loop covers entering predictions, tracking results and points each round, following Premier League fixtures and tables, and checking leaderboards. A dark, stadium-at-night look with the brand’s green keeps scores easy to scan, and a short rules walkthrough gets new players ready before kickoff.
